Visualization conventions

  • We demonstrate comparison between bnglViz that illustrates rule-based modeling as a set of individual cartoons describing interactions, and Rules Railroad that illustrates rule-based modeling as a connected network of molecules that is controlled by the rules
  • Compartments are ignored and not visualized in RRR, but are accounted for in bnglViz
  • Reaction rates and some features like functions are ignored and not visualized in both RRR and bnglViz
  • Advantage: color-agnostic: while colors are added for visual enhancement (e.g., green corresponds to forming a bond, red to breaking), the diagrams can still be read without them (by using symbols, e.g., arrows and their directions)
