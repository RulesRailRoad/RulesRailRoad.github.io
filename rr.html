Syntax diagrams

Rule-based languages such as BNGL (BioNetGen Language) consists of symbolic sentences describing molecules, species, observables, reaction rules, and other parts of BNGL file. The grammar for BNGL was described by Faeder in Extended Backus-Naur Form (EBNF). Simplified description of BNGL grammar in EBNF (with compartments and matching omitted) looks like:

Name = Letter, [{Letter|Digit|"_"}];
State = “~",(Letter|Digit),[{Letter|Digit|"_"}] | "~";
Site = Name, [{"~",State}];
Molecule = Name, ["(",[Site,[{",",Site}]],")"];
